01 Templates

Pick a look that fits you

Every template is a clean single-column layout with plain text, so ATS software can read it. Click one to see the full resume. Previews use sample content.

02 Why students use it

Built around what recruiters and ATS filters look at

ATS-friendly structure

Single-column layouts, standard section names and plain text. No tables, images or icons that confuse ATS parsers.

ATS score with a breakdown

A score out of 100 across contact info, sections, action verbs, measurable results and content depth, plus a list of what to fix.

Bullet-by-bullet suggestions

Weak bullets are rewritten in simple words using your own details. No fake numbers are added; missing results show as placeholders.

Made for B.Tech freshers

Sections for education with CGPA, projects, skills, internships and achievements, in the order recruiters expect.
